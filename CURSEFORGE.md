# Geco

Bring more variety to Minecraft's natural world with **Geco**. Discover handmade ebony trees and rare marble formations, then turn your finds into new materials for your next build.

Geco's journey begins with **Ebony Wood**, **Cream Marble**, and **Multicolor Marble**, adding new botanical and geological details while preserving vanilla terrain generation.

![Geco trees and building blocks](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_17b68524-b844-4d48-9e2a-e0f948e8019b.png)

## Ebony Trees & Wood

Explore savanna biomes to find ebony trees with several handmade shapes. Collect saplings to grow them near your home, and use their wood to expand your building palette.

The ebony set includes:

- Logs and wood, including stripped variants
- Planks, stairs, and slabs
- Fences and fence gates
- Doors and trapdoors
- Buttons and pressure plates
- Leaves and saplings

## Marble for Your Builds

Find rare **Cream Marble** and **Multicolor Marble** deposits in the Overworld. Both stone types offer a range of finishes for everything from small decorative details to grand halls:

- Natural marble
- Polished marble
- Polished marble bricks
- Polished marble tiles
- Smooth marble

Each finish includes **slabs, stairs, and walls**, giving you more ways to shape and detail your builds.

## World Generation

Geco adds its trees and marble formations to normal Minecraft terrain. In an existing world, explore newly generated chunks to discover them naturally.

## Installation & Dependencies

Available for **Minecraft 1.21.1** on **Fabric** and **NeoForge**. Choose the Geco file matching your loader and place it in your `mods` folder alongside its dependencies.

- **Architectury API** — required on both loaders
- **Fabric API** — also required on Fabric
